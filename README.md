## How to ask

Pass an Android bundle ID, such as com.openai.chatgpt, for downloads and ranking charts. The Google Play top list needs no keyword.

**Good to know:** This is Android. iOS top charts are on the App Store connector. A display name is not enough. Use the bundle ID.

## FAQ

**What identifier does Android use?**  
The bundle ID, such as com.openai.chatgpt, not the name shown in the store.

**Does this include the iOS App Store?**  
No. iOS top free and top paid are on the App Store connector.

**Is the App Downloads connector free?**  
Yes. The free plan has 100 requests a month and includes every source. No credit card. Paid plans start at $19 a month for 1,000 requests.

**Which AI apps work with it?**  
Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, Claude Code, Grok, Muse, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Windsurf and any app that supports MCP connectors.
